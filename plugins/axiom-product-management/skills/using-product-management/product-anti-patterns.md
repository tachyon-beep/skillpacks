# Product Failure Checks

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Check for: output mistaken for value; no identifiable user/problem; untestable success; missing guardrails; fabricated baselines; decisions without rationale; stale continuity; duplicated backlog; acceptance inferred from shipping; unowned risks; authority inferred from a role.

Treat these as hypotheses to inspect. Cite concrete evidence/consequence and consider counterevidence before a finding. A supported clean scope is valid.

Correct the specific failure with a bounded action or discriminating test. Do not require every checklist item to generate an artifact or force a critic disagreement.
