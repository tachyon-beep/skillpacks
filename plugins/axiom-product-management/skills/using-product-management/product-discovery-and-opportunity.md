# Opportunity Evidence

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Identify the user/task, current alternative/workaround, observed problem and cost/consequence. Distinguish direct user evidence from assumptions and requested features from underlying needs.

Compare feasible responses, including doing nothing. Choose a bounded test that could falsify the opportunity or reveal a harmful tradeoff. Use research specialists for method uncertainty, not automatic dispatch.

Record evidence provenance, affected population, uncertainties, value hypothesis and decision/reversal trigger. Do not promise business benefit from a framework label or synthetic opinions alone.
