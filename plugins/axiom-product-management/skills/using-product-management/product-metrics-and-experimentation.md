# Product Value Evidence

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Define the bet, population, success measure, source, baseline/window, guardrails and stop/reversal conditions before interpreting results. Track shipped output separately from observed user value.

Choose an experimental or observational design appropriate to the question. Check selection, pairing/dependence, exposure, instrumentation and alternative explanations; use statistical guidance where inference is consequential. No fixed sample or duration proves every product hypothesis.

Report denominator, uncertainty, missingness and confounders. A directional result may justify another bounded experiment but not a causal claim. Record the decision and owner; avoid widening a target after failure without documenting a changed bet.
