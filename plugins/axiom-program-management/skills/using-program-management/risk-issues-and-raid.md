# RAID Evidence

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Keep types distinct: risk (possible event/consequence), issue (observed problem), assumption (unverified proposition), dependency (external commitment). Link realized risk to issue and assumption failure to its affected decision.

Record ID, source/time, owner, consequence, evidence/uncertainty, action, trigger and status. A risk may use qualitative exposure or measured estimates; avoid turning illustrative probability scores into facts.

Review changes and unresolved actions at a useful cadence. Accepted risk needs actual authority/revisit conditions. A color without owner/action or a stale entry is weak evidence.
