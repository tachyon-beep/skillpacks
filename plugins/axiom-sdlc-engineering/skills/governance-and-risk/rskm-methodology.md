# Risk Management

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Record cause/event/consequence, affected assets/users, evidence/exposure, uncertainty, owner, mitigation, trigger and residual risk. Prefer actionable scenarios over a generic risk list.

Link realized risks to issues; record accepted risk with actual authority and conditions for revisiting it. Prioritize impact/exposure/recovery and dependencies, without universal probability scores or categorical domain ordering.
