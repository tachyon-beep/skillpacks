# Decision and Risk Record Templates

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

## Decision

- ID/date/status/owner and source baseline.
- Context, constraints and decision authority.
- Feasible options, criteria and evidence/uncertainty.
- Choice, rationale, consequences and reversibility.
- Implementation/verification links and revisit trigger.

## Risk or waiver

- ID/status/owner and policy obligation (if applicable).
- Cause/event/consequence, exposure evidence and uncertainty.
- Mitigation/containment, dependency and action owner.
- Actual acceptance authority, expiry/reactivation trigger.
- Closure evidence or linked realized issue.

Use an existing issue/ADR with equivalent fields; do not duplicate it in a register solely to satisfy a template.
