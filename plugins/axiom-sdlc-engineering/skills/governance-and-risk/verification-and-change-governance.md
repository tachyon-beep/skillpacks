# Verification, Acceptance and Change Governance

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Preserved from the former design/build and QA activation leaves.

## Change/configuration

Record authoritative source, baseline/build identity, review/check path, generated configuration custody, change owner and recovery constraints. File count alone does not make a decision architectural. Track debt by evidence/impact/effort/urgency; a bug ratio alone does not mandate a feature freeze.

## Verification versus acceptance

Verification checks the specified behavior/contracts. Acceptance establishes the authorized consumer's decision that the result meets the intended need. They may share evidence but neither implies the other; shipping is a separate state.

Select checks and reviewers from consequence and independence needs. No universal coverage floor, reviewer/user count, review duration or comment/finding quota applies. A clean review can be supported by coverage and limits.

## Defect/waiver disposition

Record observed failure, reproducible evidence, severity/impact, owner, status and closure check. Accepted risk/waiver needs actual authority, rationale and reactivation/expiry conditions. Link the affected requirement, test and release; do not close from prose alone.

## Standards traceability

For a selected standard, name edition and authoritative obligation, local control, evidence and owner. Distinguish local tailoring from standard requirements and assessment/certification from internal checks. Do not assert universal CMMI percentages or ROI without a source.
