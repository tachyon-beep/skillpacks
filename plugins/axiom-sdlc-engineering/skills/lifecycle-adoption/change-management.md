# Adoption Change Management

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

Identify affected people/systems, decision rights, current practices and the concrete reason for change. Preserve an accessible path for feedback and correction.

Pilot with real work; document ownership, training/support actually needed and acceptance. Communicate only through channels/actions authorized by the user. A skill does not authorize sending stakeholder messages.
