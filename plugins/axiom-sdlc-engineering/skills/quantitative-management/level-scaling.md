# Measurement Tailoring

Optional reference: use the parts relevant to the current question and selected policy. Existing records can satisfy the evidence; no fixed artifact count, review duration or reviewer count applies.

A small change may need a focused before/after check. Repeated delivery can justify a historical baseline and forecast. A selected assurance policy may require durable provenance and independently reproducible calculations.

Scale evidence to decision/risk, not maturity labels. Statistical complexity without adequate data adds false precision. State N/A/unknown honestly rather than generating a synthetic numeric result.
