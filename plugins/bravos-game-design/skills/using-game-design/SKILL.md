# Game Design

Protect the intended player experience and constraints while treating mechanics,
content and structure as replaceable. Separate observation, evidence, inference,
hypothesis and taste. Fun is contextual and requires relevant players, not a
model's confident explanation.

## Scale the work to the decision

Infer clear player/context constraints from supplied material. Ask only for
missing facts that change the design. A bounded rule, probability, timing or
parameter correction needs relevant edge closure and consequences, not a full
experience thesis or playtest package.

For invention or broad repair:

1. State who should experience what through which behavior, with observable signs
   and a plausible failure condition.
2. Map player verbs, goals, information, incentives, constraints, uncertainty,
   feedback and consequences. Diagnose existing symptoms through competing causes
   and a discriminating probe before prescribing.
3. Offer causally distinct alternatives when they improve the decision; make a
   confirmed bounded correction directly. Do not disguise tuning as new structure.
4. Trace each load-bearing mechanic through state/knowledge/capability/relationship
   change → affected player → changed future decision → session experience.
   Inspect moment, decision, loop, session and whole-game scales as scope permits.
5. Probe always/never behavior, dominance, information and consequence. Bluffing
   needs meaningful asymmetry; negotiation needs changeable terms; push-your-luck
   needs a consequential continue/stop choice. Theme is not the mechanism.
6. Check balance, player counts, waiting/excluded players, medium/facilitation and
   material accessibility, consent, social power or economic risks.
7. Test the riskiest assumption with the cheapest valid prototype. Precommit
   what supports, contradicts or leaves the claim unresolved and watch side effects.
8. Make an evidence-supported disposition: keep, rewire, simplify, shelve, kill,
   or retest unchanged. State what would reopen it. A planned test has a decision
   rule, not an already-earned result.

## Evidence limits and output

Use traces, search, simulation and synthetic play for bounded structural questions:
legal actions, closure, termination, invariants, leaks, dominance and consequence.
They cannot accept claims about human enjoyment, comprehension, trust, accessibility,
safety, bleed or willingness to return. Observe relevant people for those claims.

Return the smallest useful mechanic, playable rule, causal diagnosis, balance
model, experiment or disposition. Preserve unconventional artistic choices when
they serve the stated promise; do not normalize for assumed mass appeal.

Optional `game-design-architect` and `game-design-critic` roles support bounded
work. Compose with `bravos-systems-as-experience`, simulation, UX or narrative
skills only when the task actually crosses those disciplines.
