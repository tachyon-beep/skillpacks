# Community strategy hypotheses

Use when shared information, competition or player economies affect strategic experience. Community behavior requires observations beyond a model's imagined player personas.

## Checks

- Distinguish observed strategies/tool use from designer expectations; record sample, patch/version and relevant community context.
- Trace information access, experimentation costs, incentives and counterplay. Copying a popular build is not automatically a design failure.
- Evaluate whether shared discoveries expand agency or collapse desired choices; inspect progression and matchup conditions before calling a strategy dominant.
- Check fairness/accessibility, social coordination burdens, exploit handling and effects on less-connected players.
- Treat balance/mod/API changes as versioned interventions with possible migration and trust costs.
- Prototype or stage an intervention and observe meaningful player outcomes; external tool counts or synthetic votes alone do not establish a healthy community.

## Deliverable

Observed mechanism, competing explanation, scoped intervention and evidence/measurement plan. See [strategy](strategic-depth-from-systems.md), [sharing/mod support](modding-and-extensibility.md) and [optimization](optimization-as-play.md).
