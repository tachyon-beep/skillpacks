# Interaction hypotheses and playable evidence

Use when interacting mechanics should create meaningful player choices and consequences. Emergence is a design hypothesis, not a formula based on mechanic count.

## Checks

- State intended experience and a concrete challenge; identify the smallest mechanics/interactions that could produce it.
- Use an interaction matrix to expose isolated mechanics, repeated effects, cascade/feedback and competing solutions. Distinguish feasible interactions from reproduced behavior.
- Preserve readable cause/effect and affordances so players can form hypotheses. Unpredictability without interpretable feedback can be noise rather than discovery.
- Check dominant strategies, runaway feedback, exploits and failure recovery. A surprising exploit can be retained only when its effect supports the game and constraints.
- Compare a systemic approach with authored/hybrid alternatives; authored pacing and systemic agency can coexist.
- Prototype and test with relevant humans. Structural checks or synthetic agents can identify mechanisms but cannot establish enjoyment or memorable discoveries.

## Deliverable

Interaction hypothesis, competing explanation, bounded prototype, observable signals and human playtest evidence/plan.
