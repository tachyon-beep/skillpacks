# Optimization loops as player experience

Use when improving throughput, efficiency or resource use is itself gameplay.

## Checks

- Specify the player goal, measurable feedback and the choices that improve it. Metrics should explain cause/effect, not force one mandatory build.
- Expose bottlenecks, cost and useful comparisons without overwhelming the current task. Diagnose when the player cannot distinguish a good hypothesis from random tinkering.
- Preserve multiple tradeoffs where the design intends them: space, power, robustness, flexibility, time or style.
- Check repetitive chores, dominant blueprints, feedback delays and whether optimization still creates meaningful decisions after the first solution.
- Provide recovery/iteration tools consistent with stakes; undo, inspection and sharing may improve experimentation but change the challenge.
- Test the smallest production/efficiency loop with relevant humans; throughput diversity does not alone prove engagement.

## Deliverable

Loop hypothesis, feedback/choice prototype, competing explanation and playtest result/plan. See [strategic choices](strategic-depth-from-systems.md), [discovery](discovery-through-experimentation.md) and [community effects](community-meta-gaming.md).
