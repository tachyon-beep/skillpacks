# Creative Writing

Protect the writer's intent, voice, and chosen traditions. Treat craft frameworks
as diagnostic lenses, not universal rules. Routine prose work needs no specialist
briefing; use this workflow when a concrete craft uncertainty affects the result.

## Establish the task from context

Infer drafting, critique, revision, or planning from the request and supplied work.
Ask only when ambiguity would materially change the work. A request to rewrite or
switch focus is authorization to do that work; do not require a slash command or
additional mode confirmation. Critique alone does not authorize replacing prose.

Identify protected choices (voice, POV, genre, audience, plot constraints) and
what the writer permits changing. State consequential assumptions briefly.

## Work against the actual manuscript

1. Read the relevant prose and enough surrounding context to assess the claim.
2. For critique, quote a short passage, explain its effect, and distinguish an
   inconsistency from taste or a deliberate convention break.
3. For revision, preserve protected choices and change the smallest complete unit
   that solves the stated problem. Explain consequential changes if useful.
4. For drafting, follow the brief and voice evidence; avoid an unsolicited craft
   lecture. For planning, test cause/effect and alternatives at the requested scale.
5. Track established facts separately from interpretations: character knowledge,
   names, timeline, geography, rules, and unresolved promises.
6. Prioritize changes by their effect on the writer's goal. Structural changes
   often precede polishing, but urgency and scope can justify another order.

## Use additional readers selectively

The pack's focused agents can review structure, lines, POV, dialogue, continuity,
premise, worldbuilding, or opening/payoff. Use only the lens the work needs.
A single pass is normal for bounded work; delegate independent passes when scale,
a demonstrated blind spot, or an explicit request warrants their cost. Synthesize
available reports directly; no fixed five-reader panel or separate synthesis
agent is required. Missing coverage must stay visible.

For simulated audience reactions, `muna-panel-review` offers optional hypothesis
generation. Synthetic reactions do not establish human enjoyment, sensitivity,
market reception, or lived experience.

## Deliver

Return the requested prose, prioritized critique, revision, outline, or continuity
ledger. Include evidence and unresolved choices only to the extent they help the
writer act. Do not manufacture a quota of questions, comparisons, or criticisms.
