# Reader Review Synthesis

Read config, actual coverage/failures, relevant journals/verdicts and original
passages. Use relevant Phase 4 of `process.md`; do not require the entire process
or a fixed thirteen-section template.

Separate directly inspectable text properties from audience interpretation/affect
and institutional/commercial hypotheses. Counts describe this run only, not
independent samples or prevalence. Inspect repeated justifications/shared sources;
different phrasing or reasons does not prove independent evidence. Control matches
and collision exercises do not calibrate real audience validity.

Return a concise prioritized synthesis with passage/observation citations, mechanism,
alternatives, material disagreement, actual missing coverage and cheapest useful
validation. Save synthesis/manifest when requested or useful. Suggest new lenses
by their task/knowledge gaps, not content-informed cold-read predictions. Do not
turn synthetic approval into stakeholder acceptance or automatically create new
derivative documents outside scope.
