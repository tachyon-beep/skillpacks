# Derive Content Without Changing Its Claims

Read the actual canonical source and target reader task. Preserve material
qualifiers, numbers, units, timeframe, population, authority and approval status.
A derivative should supply essential task content, not replace it with a link.

## Select the transformation

| Mode | Contract |
|---|---|
| Distillation | Preserve argument/meaning with less detail. Length follows the task, not a percentage. |
| Translation | Re-express the same claim in the target vocabulary/frame without changing normative force. |
| Extraction | Select relevant content and make its scope clear; do not imply the omitted parts do not exist. |

A section may combine modes when it remains useful and traceable. Do not split a
coherent section merely because it uses more than a prescribed number of modes.

## Recipe

For consequential or repeated derivation, record source IDs/sections, target
reader/task, transformation, required claims/content, permitted optional links and
known ambiguity. Reuse existing manifest/claim metadata where possible.

1. Read the relevant source sections completely in context.
2. List the claims and actions the reader needs, including conditions and caveats.
3. Select/adapt content to that task. Put required information inline; retain source
   references for evidence and optional depth.
4. Compare material claims with the source. Do not round away precision, strengthen
   may to will, turn partial into complete, or imply proposed work is approved.
5. Label any new inference, priority or recommendation separately and supply its
   basis. An executive-risk conclusion is new analysis, not faithful compression
   merely because it sounds useful.
6. Check links, definitions and task closure at the target depth. State untested
   comprehension and source conflicts rather than inventing certainty.

## Example

Source: "Four of six controls are partially addressed in the sampled deployment."
Faithful derivative: "In the sampled deployment, four of six controls are partly
addressed; the remaining coverage still needs assessment."
Unfaithful: "Existing controls address most failures." It drops sampling and
partial coverage and changes controls into failures.

## Deferral review

Classify each parent link: optional evidence/depth, or essential content missing
from the derivative. Only the latter needs inline synthesis. Cross-links and brief
sections are not inherently lazy or defective. Match detail to the actual task.

A synthetic reader probe can find possible omissions. It does not establish human
comprehension or stakeholder acceptance. For source changes use
[document-evolution.md](document-evolution.md); for registry fidelity use
[cross-document-consistency.md](cross-document-consistency.md).
