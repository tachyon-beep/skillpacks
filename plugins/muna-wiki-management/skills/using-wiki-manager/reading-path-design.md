# Reader Task Paths

Design paths from actual tasks and available audience evidence. Inferred personas
are hypotheses; job title alone does not establish knowledge, reading rate or need.

## Minimal reader record

Record ID, task, relevant knowledge, constraints/access, entry point and terminal
outcome. Optional depth/time labels help when they reflect real needs. Use enough
roles to cover meaningful differences; there is no persona quota.

Map entry → prerequisite/context → relevant action/decision → expected outcome.
Check that necessary concepts are available in the path and links resolve. Optional
depth may branch; do not force a reader into a larger root just to recover missing
essential instructions. Preserve published anchors/routes when changing topology.

A coverage matrix can map reader task × needed topic to actual sections or an
explicit out-of-scope reason. Build it only when it helps expose consequential
coverage gaps; not every persona needs every topic. Unvisited content may serve
another audience or reference task and is not automatically unnecessary.

## Validate

Trace the path against original content and available mechanical link checks.
Estimate reading budgets cautiously; actual task/reading trials are stronger than
a generic words-per-minute formula. Do not require fixed 5/30/deep-dive paths for
every reader. Record assumptions and what real users completed or could not find.

Return a path/label/navigation correction or prioritized missing information with
source locations and affected task. Use [document-set-architecture.md](document-set-architecture.md)
for lineage and [document-governance.md](document-governance.md) for ownership.
