# Feedback patterns as hypotheses

Use when recurrent behavior or side effects suggest accumulation, delays or feedback. Repetition alone does not establish a systems explanation.

## Checks

- Record observed behavior over time and the attempted interventions; distinguish recurrence, oscillation, saturation and growth/decline from anecdotes.
- Identify candidate stocks, inflows/outflows, capacity limits, reinforcing/balancing links and delays.
- Test direct/local causes and alternative mechanisms; do not force the case into an archetype.
- Keep an evidence label for each link and parameter. A familiar story can explain observations while still being wrong.
- Choose the smallest causal/stock-flow model that changes the decision. Use qualitative uncertainty when numerical calibration is unavailable.
- State an observation, instrumented trace or bounded intervention that would discriminate between explanations.

## Deliverable

Observed pattern, competing mechanisms, evidence/gaps and discriminating next step. Optional [archetypes](systems-archetypes-reference.md) provide vocabulary; [causal maps](causal-loop-diagramming.md) and [models](stocks-and-flows-modeling.md) provide explicit structure.
