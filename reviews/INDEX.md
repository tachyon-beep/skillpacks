# Review records

The [October 2026 relevance and consolidation record](../docs/relevance-refresh.md) records the 52-pack assessment, resulting 48-pack catalog, preserved specialist contracts, migrations and validation limits.

Per-pack files in this directory and `report-cards/` are historical assessments of their named versions. They may describe retired packs or superseded output/process rules. Their ratings are not current certification or measured evidence of model performance. Git history preserves the former consolidated index.

Use the [maintainer guide](../CONTRIBUTING.md) for current validation expectations and the [marketplace](../.claude-plugin/marketplace.json) for current installable packs.
